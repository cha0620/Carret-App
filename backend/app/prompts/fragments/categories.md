# Categories (choose EXACTLY one per finding)

## surface_damage
Physical wear or damage that is NOT part of the original design.
- includes: stains (coffee/oil/ink), scratches, scuffs, tears, holes,
  dents, cracks, fraying, pilling, discoloration, yellowing, dirt,
  wear creases, faded patches
- NOT: printed graphics, intentional distressing, seams, shadows,
  normal fabric texture

## functional_fault
Evidence that a part does not work as intended.
- includes: display lines, dead pixels, screen crack affecting display,
  broken or missing buttons, bent ports, cracked camera lens
- NOT: purely cosmetic issues (→ surface_damage)

## print
Anything intentionally printed, embroidered, or attached by the maker.
- includes: logos, brand symbols, emblems, printed text, slogans,
  embroidered letters, labels, tags, decorative graphics/patterns
- NOT: stains or dirt that merely resemble text/logo

## other
Anything visible that fits none of the above
(watermark, background object, accessory, hangtag string).
