Also judge the TEXT physically on the item (ignore watermarks, captions and UI overlays
laid over the photo, and text on the background or other objects):
- "none": no readable text, logo lettering, numbers or scale marks on the item at all
- "simple": a few large, clearly readable marks — e.g. one or two brand logos,
  a model name, up to about 3 short words or lines
- "dense": a lot of SMALL text — spec or ingredient labels with more than about 5 lines,
  dials or scales covered with numbers, fine engraving
When unsure, choose "simple". If you report any "print" finding, text_level is not "none".
